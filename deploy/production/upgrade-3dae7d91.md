observer. Verify the final retained-row seal, remove the observer Deployment,

Restore the original active source settings and full authority, honor existing

Require both MCP revisions, IPv4/IPv6 HTTP and native WebTransport, supported SSE
compression, private boundary negatives, fixed capture bytes, complete notices
and matching corresponding source. Active qualification includes fresh early and
late proofs, at least 32 samples over 900 monotonic seconds, gaps at most 45

checkpoint, S3-binding and reserve checks remain non-waivable. Reconcile lost
rename responses by journalled path/inode identity; never replay a consumed
